# Nik Vassev
> Brand Strategist for visionary startups.

Nik Vassev is a multi-time exited tech founder, brand strategist and growth marketer, and the Co-founder and Managing Director of Storyline Pros. He founded Guide IQ, had a health tech exit, and has built startups that generated tens of millions in revenue. He has helped hundreds of tech startups with brand strategy and growth marketing, writes for Entrepreneur.com (formerly Forbes), and publishes the weekly newsletter Systems Over Hustle.
